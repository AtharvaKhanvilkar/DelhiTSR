DEED OF RELINQUISHMENT
(By way of Family Settlement / Family Arrangement)
================================================================================
This DEED OF RELINQUISHMENT ("Deed") is executed at New Delhi on this 10th day
of January, 2024.
BETWEEN:
MR. VIKRAM SINGH CHAUHAN, Son of Late Shri Harbhajan Singh Chauhan, aged about
52 years, an Indian citizen, Hindu by religion, residing at Flat No. 8, Fourth
Floor, Plot No. 1A, Hauz Khas Enclave, New Delhi – 110016, PAN: BKCPC8834M,
Aadhaar No. XXXX-XXXX-3361 (hereinafter referred to as the "RELEASOR", which
expression shall, unless repugnant to the context or meaning thereof, mean and
include his heirs, legal representatives, executors, administrators, successors
and assigns) of the FIRST PART;
AND
MRS. PRIYA VIKRAM CHAUHAN, Wife of Mr. Vikram Singh Chauhan, aged about 47
years, an Indian citizen, Hindu by religion, residing at Flat No. 8, Fourth
Floor, Plot No. 1A, Hauz Khas Enclave, New Delhi – 110016, PAN: CJKPC2294L,
Aadhaar No. XXXX-XXXX-6738 (hereinafter referred to as the "RELEASEE", which
expression shall, unless repugnant to the context or meaning thereof, mean and
include her heirs, legal representatives, executors, administrators, successors
and assigns) of the SECOND PART.
The Releasor and the Releasee are hereinafter individually referred to as "Party"
and collectively referred to as "Parties".
RECITALS
WHEREAS the Releasor and the Releasee are husband and wife, both professing the
Hindu religion, and are governed by the provisions of the Hindu Succession Act,
1956, the Hindu Marriage Act, 1955, and the Hindu Minority and Guardianship Act,
1956, as applicable;
AND WHEREAS the property more fully described in the Schedule hereunder, being
Flat No. 8, situated on the Fourth Floor, Plot No. 1A, Hauz Khas Enclave, New
Delhi – 110016, admeasuring approximately 1200 sq.ft. (super built-up area),
was originally acquired by Late Shri Harbhajan Singh Chauhan, Father of the
Releasor, by virtue of an allotment from the Delhi Development Authority /
original builder-developer, details of which are on record with the concerned
authorities;
AND WHEREAS Late Shri Harbhajan Singh Chauhan, during his lifetime, executed a
registered Gift Deed bearing Document No. 2891/2015 dated 15th September 2015,
registered at the Office of the Sub-Registrar, SRO V-A (Hauz Khas), New Delhi,
whereby he gifted and transferred the said Flat No. 8 to Mrs. Priya Vikram
Chauhan (the Releasee herein) absolutely and irrevocably, and the said Gift Deed
was duly accepted by the Releasee and possession was delivered to her;
AND WHEREAS Late Shri Harbhajan Singh Chauhan passed away on the 22nd day of
March, 2023, leaving behind his son, Mr. Vikram Singh Chauhan (Releasor herein),
and the Releasee, as his surviving family members;
AND WHEREAS upon the demise of Late Shri Harbhajan Singh Chauhan, certain
questions arose within the family regarding whether the Releasor, being the
natural son and a Class I legal heir under Section 8 of the Hindu Succession Act,
1956, read with the Schedule thereto, may have any residual, reversionary, or
contingent right, title, or interest in the said property notwithstanding the
prior Gift Deed in favour of the Releasee;
AND WHEREAS the Parties, after due deliberation and with the assistance and
counsel of elders of the family and independent legal advice, have arrived at a
bona fide family settlement and arrangement, whereby the Releasor has agreed to
release, relinquish, and forever give up any and all rights, title, interest,
claim, or demand, whether present, future, contingent, residual, reversionary,
or otherwise, that he may have in or over the said property in favour of the
Releasee, so as to confirm and perfect the Releasee's absolute ownership;
AND WHEREAS this Deed of Relinquishment is being executed pursuant to the said
family settlement and arrangement and is without any monetary consideration, the
same being an act of love, affection, and familial harmony;
AND WHEREAS this Deed is executed on non-judicial stamp paper of the appropriate
value as prescribed under the Indian Stamp Act, 1899, and the Delhi Stamp
(Prevention of Under-Valuation of Instruments) Rules, 2007, as applicable;
AND WHEREAS this family arrangement and settlement is recognised and upheld under
Indian law, as reiterated by the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India in Kale & Ors.
v. Deputy Director of Consolidation & Ors., AIR 1976 SC 807, and other judicial
pronouncements, and does not require consideration to be valid.
NOW THIS DEED OF RELINQUISHMENT WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:
1. In pursuance of the aforesaid family settlement and arrangement, and in
consideration of the natural love and affection that the Releasor bears
towards the Releasee and for the purpose of maintaining peace, harmony, and
goodwill within the family, the Releasor hereby voluntarily, unconditionally,
and irrevocably RELEASES, RELINQUISHES, RENOUNCES, ABANDONS, AND GIVES UP
all his right, title, interest, claim, and demand, whether legal, equitable,
actual, contingent, residual, reversionary, expectant, or of any nature
whatsoever, in and over the property described in the Schedule hereunder,
being Flat No. 8, Fourth Floor, Plot No. 1A, Hauz Khas Enclave, New Delhi –
110016, IN FAVOUR OF the Releasee, Mrs. Priya Vikram Chauhan, her heirs,
successors, and assigns, absolutely and forever.
2. The Releasor hereby declares and confirms that the Releasee, Mrs. Priya
Vikram Chauhan, is and shall henceforth be the sole, absolute, and
undisputed owner of the said property, with full right to hold, possess,
enjoy, sell, mortgage, lease, gift, transfer, or otherwise deal with the
same in any manner she deems fit, without any interference, obstruction, or
objection from the Releasor or anyone claiming through or under him.
3. The Releasor declares that he has not created or permitted to be created any
encumbrance, charge, lien, mortgage, or third-party right of any nature
whatsoever over the said property, and the property is free from all
encumbrances, attachments, and claims of whatsoever nature.
4. The Releasor hereby covenants and warrants as follows:
(a) That the Releasor has the legal competency and authority to execute this
Deed and to release and relinquish his rights in the manner set out
herein;
(b) That the Releasor has executed this Deed out of his own free will, with
full knowledge and understanding of the nature and consequences of this
relinquishment, and without any coercion, undue influence, fraud,
misrepresentation, or compulsion from any quarter;
(c) That the Releasor shall not at any time hereafter make any claim, raise
any objection, or institute any suit, proceeding, or action in any
court, tribunal, or authority against the Releasee or her successors in
respect of the said property;
(d) That the Releasor shall, at his own cost and at the request of the
Releasee, execute and register all further deeds, documents, and
instruments as may be necessary or expedient to give full effect to this
Deed and to perfect the title of the Releasee;
(e) That in the event any person claiming through or under the Releasor
challenges or disputes the right, title, or ownership of the Releasee
in respect of the said property, the Releasor shall defend, indemnify,
and hold harmless the Releasee against all claims, costs, damages, and
expenses arising therefrom.
5. The Releasee hereby accepts this relinquishment and acknowledges the same.
6. The Parties declare that this Deed is executed without any monetary
consideration and is a bona fide family arrangement. Stamp duty has been
paid in accordance with Article 55 of Schedule I-A to the Indian Stamp Act,
1899, as applicable in the National Capital Territory of Delhi.
7. This Deed shall be binding upon the heirs, executors, administrators, legal
representatives, successors, and assigns of both Parties.
8. This Deed shall be governed by the laws of India, and the courts at New
Delhi shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
9. The Releasor confirms that he has received independent legal advice prior to
the execution of this Deed and has fully understood the legal implications
of the relinquishment effected hereby.
