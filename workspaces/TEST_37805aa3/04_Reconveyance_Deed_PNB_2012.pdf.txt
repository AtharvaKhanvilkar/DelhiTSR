The Releasor and the Releasee are hereinafter individually referred to

W H E R E A S
A. The Releasee is the sole and absolute owner of ALL THAT residential
flat being Flat No. 8, situated on the Fourth Floor of the building
erected on Plot No. 1A, Hauz Khas Enclave, New Delhi - 110016,
admeasuring approximately 1200 (One Thousand Two Hundred) square feet
of super built-up area, more fully described in the Schedule of
Property hereunder written (hereinafter referred to as the "Said
Property" or the "Said Flat").
B. The Releasee had acquired the Said Flat by virtue of a Sale Deed
bearing Document No. 5847 of the year 2005, registered at the Office
of the Sub-Registrar, SRO V-A (Hauz Khas), New Delhi.
C. The Releasee had obtained a Home Improvement Loan of Rs. 35,00,000/-
(Rupees Thirty-Five Lakhs Only) from the Releasor and had, as
security for repayment of the said loan, executed a Mortgage Deed
(Simple Mortgage) dated 10th August 2008, bearing Document
No. 9234 of the year 2008, registered at the Office of the
Sub-Registrar, SRO V-A (Hauz Khas), New Delhi on 12th August 2008
(hereinafter referred to as the "Original Mortgage Deed"), whereby
the Releasee had mortgaged the Said Property in favour of the
Releasor by way of simple mortgage under Section 58(b) of the
Transfer of Property Act, 1882.
D. The principal terms of the Original Mortgage Deed were:
(i) Principal Loan Amount : Rs. 35,00,000/- (Rupees Thirty-
Five Lakhs Only)
(ii) Rate of Interest : 10.25% per annum (floating),
linked to BPLR
(iii) Loan Tenure : 180 months (15 years)
(iv) EMI : Rs. 38,247/- per month
E. The Releasee has fully repaid and discharged the entire loan amount,
together with all interest, charges, costs, and other monies due and
payable to the Releasor under the Original Mortgage Deed and the
terms of the loan sanction. The final payment was received by the
Releasor on 22nd October 2012, and the Releasor's Loan Account
No. 3125001500124987 (South Extension Branch) now stands at NIL
balance. The Releasor has issued a No Dues Certificate / Loan
Closure Certificate dated 25th October 2012 vide Reference
No. AXIS/SE/NDC/2012/5891 confirming the full and final repayment
and closure of the loan account.
F. Consequent upon the full and final repayment and discharge of the
loan, the Releasor has agreed to release and reconvey the Mortgaged
Property to the Releasee, to vacate the mortgage, and to return all
original title documents deposited with the Bank, so that the Said
Property stands freed, released and discharged from the mortgage
created by the Original Mortgage Deed.
G. The Releasor has also initiated the process of filing satisfaction
of charge with the Central Registry of Securitisation Asset
Reconstruction and Security Interest of India (CERSAI) in respect
of the mortgage registered thereunder.
NOW THIS RECONVEYANCE DEED / DEED OF RELEASE WITNESSETH
AS FOLLOWS:
1. RELEASE AND RECONVEYANCE
In consideration of the full and final repayment by the Releasee of
all sums due and payable under the Original Mortgage Deed dated
10th August 2008, Document No. 9234/2008, and in pursuance of the
agreement between the Parties, the Releasor HEREBY RELEASES,
RECONVEYS, REASSIGNS AND DISCHARGES unto the Releasee the
residential flat described in the Schedule of Property hereunder
written, TO HAVE AND TO HOLD the same unto the Releasee absolutely
and forever, freed and discharged from the simple mortgage created
by the Original Mortgage Deed and from all claims, rights, title,
interest, lien and encumbrances of the Releasor thereunder.
2. VACATION OF MORTGAGE
The mortgage created by the Original Mortgage Deed bearing Document
No. 9234 of the year 2008, registered at the Office of the
Sub-Registrar, SRO V-A (Hauz Khas), New Delhi, is hereby declared
to be fully satisfied, vacated, released and discharged with effect
from 22nd October 2012, being the date of final repayment.
3. RETURN OF TITLE DOCUMENTS
The Releasor hereby confirms that all original title documents
pertaining to the Said Property, which were deposited with the Bank
as security, have been returned to the Releasee on this date,
including but not limited to:
(a) Original Sale Deed No. 5847/2005, registered at SRO V-A
(Hauz Khas), New Delhi;
(b) Original Allotment Letter;
(c) Original Possession Letter;
(d) Approved Building Plans and Completion/Occupancy Certificate;
(e) Mutation records and property tax receipts;
(f) Share Certificate of the Cooperative Group Housing Society /
Residents Welfare Association (if applicable);
(g) All other documents and papers pertaining to the Said Property
that were in the custody of the Bank.
The Releasee acknowledges receipt of all the aforesaid original
documents.
4. COVENANTS OF THE RELEASOR
The Releasor hereby covenants with the Releasee as follows:
4.1 NO FURTHER CLAIM: The Releasor hereby confirms that after the
execution and registration of this Deed, the Releasor shall
have no further right, title, interest, claim, demand, or lien
of any nature whatsoever in, to, or over the Said Property or
any part thereof by virtue of the Original Mortgage Deed or
otherwise.
4.2 FULL DISCHARGE: The Releasor confirms and acknowledges that the
Releasee has fully and finally repaid and discharged the entire
loan amount together with all interest, costs, charges, penal
interest (if any), and all other monies due under the Original
Mortgage Deed and the terms of the loan sanction, and nothing
further remains due or payable by the Releasee to the Releasor
on account of the said loan or the Original Mortgage Deed.
4.3 NO IMPEDIMENT: The Releasor shall not do or cause to be done
anything that may in any manner impede, obstruct, or interfere
with the free and absolute enjoyment and disposal of the Said
Property by the Releasee, and the Releasor shall, at the
request and cost of the Releasee, execute and register such
further deeds and documents as may be reasonably required to
give full effect to this release.
4.4 CERSAI SATISFACTION: The Releasor undertakes to file the
satisfaction of charge / closure of security interest with
CERSAI within thirty (30) days from the date of execution of
this Deed, in accordance with the provisions of the SARFAESI
Act, 2002 and the rules made thereunder.
4.5 INDEMNITY: The Releasor shall indemnify and hold harmless the
Releasee against any loss, damage, cost, charge, or expense
that the Releasee may suffer or incur on account of the
Releasor's failure to comply with any of its obligations under
this Deed, including the failure to file satisfaction of charge
with CERSAI.
5. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF THE RELEASOR
The Releasor hereby represents and warrants:
5.1 That the Releasor has full power and authority to execute this
Deed and to release and reconvey the Said Property to the
Releasee.
5.2 That the Original Mortgage Deed dated 10th August 2008, being
Document No. 9234/2008, is the only mortgage or charge created
by the Releasee in favour of the Releasor on the Said Property,
and there exists no other mortgage, charge, lien, or
encumbrance in favour of the Releasor on the Said Property.
5.3 That the Releasor has not assigned, transferred or securitised
the loan or the security interest created under the Original
Mortgage Deed to any third party.
5.4 That no proceedings under the SARFAESI Act, 2002 or otherwise
are pending or contemplated by the Releasor against the
Releasee or the Said Property.
6. STAMP DUTY AND REGISTRATION
This Reconveyance Deed / Deed of Release is executed on non-judicial
stamp paper of appropriate value as prescribed under the Indian
Stamp Act, 1899, as applicable in the National Capital Territory of
Delhi. All stamp duty and registration charges in connection with
this Deed shall be borne by the Releasee. This Deed shall be
registered at the Office of the Sub-Registrar, SRO V-A (Hauz Khas),
New Delhi, in accordance with Section 17 of the Registration Act,
1908.
7. MISCELLANEOUS
7.1 GOVERNING LAW: This Deed shall be governed by and construed in
accordance with the laws of India. The courts at New Delhi
shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
7.2 COUNTERPARTS: This Deed may be executed in two or more
counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and
all of which together shall constitute one and the same
instrument.
7.3 EFFECTIVE DATE: This Deed shall be effective from the date of
its registration at the Office of the Sub-Registrar.
