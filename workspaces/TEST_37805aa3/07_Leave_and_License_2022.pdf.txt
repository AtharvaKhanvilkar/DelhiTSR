LEAVE AND LICENSE AGREEMENT
================================================================================

RECITALS
WHEREAS the Licensor is the absolute and undisputed owner of the property more
fully described in the Schedule hereunder, having acquired the same by virtue of
a registered Gift Deed bearing Document No. 2891/2015 dated 15th September 2015,
registered at the Office of the Sub-Registrar, SRO V-A (Hauz Khas), New Delhi,
and the Licensor has been in peaceful possession and enjoyment of the said
property since the date of the said Gift Deed;
AND WHEREAS the said property is a self-contained residential flat, being Flat
No. 8, situated on the Fourth Floor, Plot No. 1A, Hauz Khas Enclave, New Delhi
– 110016, admeasuring approximately 1200 sq.ft. (super built-up area), free from
all encumbrances, charges, liens, attachments, and litigations of whatsoever
nature;
AND WHEREAS the Licensee has approached the Licensor and requested her to grant
leave and license to occupy and use the said flat for a temporary period, and the
Licensor has agreed to grant such leave and license on the terms and conditions
hereinafter appearing;
AND WHEREAS the Parties have mutually agreed that this Agreement shall be a Leave
and License under and in accordance with Section 52 of the Indian Easements Act,
1882, and shall NOT constitute a lease or tenancy under the Transfer of Property
Act, 1882, or the Delhi Rent Control Act, 1958, or any other applicable law, and
the Licensee shall have no right, title, or interest in the said property and
shall not claim any tenancy or sub-tenancy rights therein;
AND WHEREAS this Agreement is being executed on non-judicial stamp paper of
appropriate value as prescribed under the Indian Stamp Act, 1899, read with the
Delhi Stamp (Prevention of Under-Valuation of Instruments) Rules, 2007.
NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AND IT IS HEREBY AGREED BY AND BETWEEN THE
PARTIES AS FOLLOWS:
1. GRANT OF LICENSE: The Licensor hereby grants to the Licensee a revocable
license to use and occupy the premises described in the Schedule below,
namely Flat No. 8, Fourth Floor, Plot No. 1A, Hauz Khas Enclave, New Delhi
– 110016, strictly on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.
The Licensor retains legal possession and ownership of the said premises at
all times.
2. LICENSE PERIOD: The license hereby granted shall be for a fixed period of
Eleven (11) months commencing from the 1st day of April, 2022 and expiring
on the 28th day of February, 2023 (both days inclusive), unless terminated
earlier in accordance with the provisions herein.
3. LICENSE FEE: The Licensee shall pay to the Licensor a monthly license fee of
Rs. 35,000/- (Rupees Thirty-Five Thousand Only) payable in advance on or
before the 5th day of each calendar month. The first month's license fee
shall be payable on the date of execution of this Agreement. Payments shall
be made by cheque drawn on a scheduled bank or by electronic bank transfer
(NEFT/RTGS/UPI) to the Licensor's bank account, details of which have been
shared separately.
4. SECURITY DEPOSIT: The Licensee has, prior to the execution of this
Agreement, paid to the Licensor a refundable interest-free security deposit
of Rs. 2,00,000/- (Rupees Two Lakhs Only) by way of demand draft /
electronic transfer. The receipt of the said amount is hereby acknowledged
by the Licensor. The said deposit shall be refunded by the Licensor to the
Licensee within thirty (30) days of the expiry or earlier termination of
this Agreement and the Licensee handing over vacant and peaceful possession
of the said premises in the same condition as at the time of taking
possession (fair wear and tear excepted), subject to deduction of any
outstanding license fee, utility charges, damages (beyond normal wear and
tear), or any other amounts due from the Licensee under this Agreement.
5. PURPOSE AND USE: The Licensee shall use the said premises as commercial
office space for his consultancy firm and shall not use the same or any part
thereof for any unlawful, immoral, or objectionable purpose. The Licensee
shall not store any hazardous material, inflammable substances, or
contraband within the premises.
6. MAINTENANCE AND OUTGOINGS: The Licensee shall bear and pay all charges on
account of maintenance of the said flat, including the monthly maintenance
charges payable to the Residents' Welfare Association / Cooperative Group
Housing Society, during the license period. Additionally, the Licensee
shall pay all charges for electricity (as per the individual meter installed
for the flat), water, piped gas, and any other utilities consumed by the
Licensee during the license period. Property tax and any structural
insurance premium shall be borne by the Licensor.
7. SUBLETTING AND ASSIGNMENT: The Licensee shall NOT sublet, assign, transfer,
or part with the possession or occupancy of the said premises or any part
thereof to any third party, whether with or without consideration, without
the prior written consent of the Licensor. Any such unauthorised subletting
or assignment shall render this Agreement void and the Licensee shall be
liable to forthwith vacate the premises.
8. ALTERATIONS AND MODIFICATIONS: The Licensee shall not make any structural
alterations, modifications, additions, or improvements to the said premises
without the prior written consent of the Licensor. Any permissible
non-structural changes (such as drilling for curtain rods, installation of
air-conditioning units in designated locations) shall be restored by the
Licensee at the time of vacating the premises at the Licensee's own cost.
9. CONDITION OF PREMISES: The Licensee acknowledges having inspected the said
premises and having found the same in good and habitable condition. The
Licensee shall maintain the premises in the same condition throughout the
license period (fair wear and tear excepted) and shall hand over vacant
possession in the same condition at the time of vacating.
10. REPAIR AND MAINTENANCE: Minor day-to-day repairs, including repairs to
sanitary fittings, electrical fittings, switches, taps, and similar items
up to a value of Rs. 5,000/- per instance, shall be borne by the Licensee.
Major structural repairs and repairs exceeding Rs. 5,000/- per instance
shall be the responsibility of the Licensor, provided the Licensee gives
prompt written notice thereof.
11. TERMINATION BY LICENSOR: The Licensor may revoke this license at any time
during the license period by giving the Licensee not less than two (2)
months' prior written notice. Upon receipt of such notice, the Licensee
shall vacate the premises and hand over vacant possession on or before the
date specified in the notice. In such event, the security deposit shall be
refunded as per Clause 4 above.
12. TERMINATION BY LICENSEE: The Licensee may terminate this Agreement at any
time during the license period by giving the Licensor not less than two (2)
months' prior written notice. Upon such termination, the Licensee shall
vacate and hand over vacant possession, and the Licensor shall refund the
security deposit as per Clause 4 above.
13. DEFAULT AND CONSEQUENCES: If the Licensee fails to pay the monthly license
fee for two (2) consecutive months, or commits a breach of any material
term of this Agreement, the Licensor shall be entitled to revoke this
license forthwith and require the Licensee to vacate the premises within
fifteen (15) days of written notice of such revocation. In such event, the
Licensor may deduct from the security deposit any arrears of license fee and
other dues.
14. LICENSOR'S RIGHT OF INSPECTION: The Licensor, or her authorised agent,
shall have the right to enter and inspect the said premises at any
reasonable time upon giving reasonable prior notice to the Licensee. The
Licensee shall not obstruct or deny such access.
15. INVENTORY AND FIXTURES: An inventory list of all fixtures, fittings,
furniture, and appliances provided by the Licensor with the premises has
been prepared in duplicate, signed by both Parties, and annexed hereto as
Annexure-A. The Licensee shall be responsible for the safety and upkeep of
the items listed therein.
16. INSURANCE: The Licensor shall maintain adequate insurance for the structure
of the building. The Licensee shall, at his own cost, insure his personal
belongings, equipment, and contents kept in the premises. Neither Party
shall be liable for loss or damage to the other's property except to the
extent caused by the negligence or wilful act of such Party.
17. COMPLIANCE WITH LAWS: The Licensee shall comply with all applicable laws,
rules, regulations, and bye-laws of the local authority, the Residents'
Welfare Association, and any other competent authority in relation to the
use and occupation of the said premises.
18. FORCE MAJEURE: Neither Party shall be liable for delay or failure in
performance of its obligations hereunder to the extent that such delay or
failure is caused by circumstances beyond the reasonable control of that
Party, including acts of God, natural disasters, epidemics, government
restrictions, or war.
19. DISPUTE RESOLUTION: All disputes and differences arising out of or in
connection with this Agreement shall first be attempted to be resolved
amicably by mutual discussion. If the Parties fail to resolve the dispute
within thirty (30) days, the matter shall be referred to a sole arbitrator
mutually appointed by both Parties, in accordance with the Arbitration and
Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat of arbitration shall be New Delhi, and the
proceedings shall be conducted in English.
20. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION: This Agreement shall be governed by and
construed in accordance with the laws of India, and the courts at New Delhi
shall have exclusive jurisdiction in relation to any disputes arising out of
this Agreement.
21. GENERAL PROVISIONS:
(a) This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with
respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior
negotiations, discussions, and agreements between them.
(b) Any amendment or modification to this Agreement shall be valid only if
made in writing and signed by both Parties.
(c) The headings and clause numbers in this Agreement are for convenience
of reference only and shall not affect the interpretation hereof.
(d) If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or
unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force
and effect.
(e) This Agreement is executed in duplicate; the original shall be retained
by the Licensor and the duplicate by the Licensee.
(f) The Licensee acknowledges that this is a Leave and License and NOT a
lease, and the Licensee shall acquire no right of tenancy or any estate
or interest in the property.
