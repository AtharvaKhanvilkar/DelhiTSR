MR. VIKRAM SINGH CHAUHAN, son of Shri Harbhajan Singh Chauhan, aged about

AND
AXIS BANK, a banking company incorporated under the Companies Act, 1956, having

New Delhi - 110049, represented herein by its authorized officer
MR. SURESH BAHADUR, Chief Manager, duly authorized vide Board Resolution
dated 22nd July 2008 (hereinafter referred to as the "MORTGAGEE" or the
"BANK", which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context or
meaning thereof, include its successors and assigns) of the SECOND PART.
The MORTGAGOR and the MORTGAGEE are hereinafter individually referred to
as the "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."
W H E R E A S
A. The Mortgagor is the sole and absolute owner in possession of ALL
THAT residential flat being Flat No. 8 situated on the Fourth Floor
of the building erected on Plot No. 1A, Hauz Khas Enclave, New
Delhi - 110016, admeasuring approximately 1200 (One Thousand Two
Hundred) square feet of super built-up area, more fully described in
the Schedule of Property hereunder written (hereinafter referred to
as the "Mortgaged Property" or the "Said Flat").
B. The Mortgagor acquired the Said Flat by virtue of a Sale Deed
bearing Document No. 5847 of the year 2005, registered at the Office
of the Sub-Registrar, SRO V-A (Hauz Khas), New Delhi, executed in
his favour and the same has been in his continuous, peaceful and
uninterrupted possession and enjoyment since the date of purchase.
C. The Mortgagor has applied to the Mortgagee for a Home Improvement
Loan of Rs. 35,00,000/- (Rupees Thirty-Five Lakhs Only) for the
purpose of renovation, upgradation and structural improvement of the
Said Flat.
D. The Mortgagee, after carrying out due diligence, valuation of the
Mortgaged Property by its approved valuer, verification of the title
documents, and being satisfied with the credit-worthiness and
repayment capacity of the Mortgagor, has sanctioned a Home
Improvement Loan of Rs. 35,00,000/- (Rupees Thirty-Five Lakhs Only)
vide Sanction Letter Ref. No. AXIS/SE/HL/2008/1437 dated 28th July
2008, on the following principal terms:
(i) Principal Loan Amount : Rs. 35,00,000/- (Rupees Thirty-
Five Lakhs Only)
(ii) Rate of Interest : 10.25% per annum (floating),
linked to the Bank's Prime Lending Rate (BPLR) as
applicable from time to time
(iii) Loan Tenure : 180 (One Hundred and Eighty)
months, i.e. 15 (Fifteen) years
(iv) EMI : Rs. 38,247/- (Rupees Thirty-Eight
Thousand Two Hundred and Forty-Seven Only) per month
(v) Mode of Repayment : Equated Monthly Installments (EMI)
through auto-debit from the Mortgagor's Savings Account
No. 3125001500124987 maintained at the South Extension
Branch of the Bank
E. The Mortgagor has agreed to create a simple mortgage on the Said
Flat in favour of the Mortgagee as security for repayment of the
aforesaid loan together with interest, costs, charges, and all other
monies that may become due and payable under the terms of the loan.
F. The Parties have agreed to reduce the terms of the mortgage into
writing.
NOW THIS MORTGAGE DEED WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:
1. OPERATIVE MORTGAGE CLAUSE
In consideration of the sum of Rs. 35,00,000/- (Rupees Thirty-Five
Lakhs Only) lent and advanced by the Mortgagee to the Mortgagor
(receipt whereof the Mortgagor hereby acknowledges), the Mortgagor
hereby mortgages by way of Simple Mortgage under Section 58(b) of the
Transfer of Property Act, 1882, the residential flat described in the
Schedule of Property hereunder written, and the Mortgagor doth
hereby bind himself personally to repay the said principal sum of
Rs. 35,00,000/- together with interest thereon at the rate of 10.25%
per annum (floating, linked to BPLR) and all other monies due
hereunder, by paying monthly EMIs of Rs. 38,247/- each on or before
the 5th day of each calendar month, commencing from 5th September
2008, and agrees that in the event of his failure to pay the
mortgage money as herein provided, the Mortgagee shall have the
right to cause the Mortgaged Property to be sold and the proceeds
of the sale to be applied in payment of the mortgage money.
2. COVENANTS OF THE MORTGAGOR
The Mortgagor hereby covenants with the Mortgagee as follows:
2.1 REPAYMENT: The Mortgagor shall punctually and regularly pay
each monthly EMI of Rs. 38,247/- and all other sums due under
the loan on or before the respective due dates without any
default, demand, protest or deduction.
2.2 INTEREST RATE VARIATION: The Mortgagor acknowledges that the
rate of interest is floating and linked to the BPLR of the
Bank. Any change in the BPLR shall automatically result in a
corresponding change in the applicable rate of interest. The
Bank shall have the right to adjust the EMI amount or the loan
tenure or both, as it may deem fit, upon any such change.
2.3 INSURANCE: The Mortgagor shall, at his own cost and expense,
keep the Mortgaged Property adequately insured against fire,
earthquake, flood, and such other risks as the Mortgagee may
reasonably require, for the full market value of the property
or the outstanding loan amount, whichever is higher, in the
joint names of the Mortgagor and the Mortgagee, and shall
punctually pay all premia and produce the policy and renewal
receipts to the Bank when required.
2.4 NO FURTHER ENCUMBRANCE: The Mortgagor shall not, without the
prior written consent of the Mortgagee, create any second
mortgage, charge, lien, or encumbrance of any kind whatsoever
on the Mortgaged Property or any part thereof, nor shall he
sell, transfer, assign, lease, or otherwise dispose of the
same during the subsistence of this mortgage.
2.5 MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS: The Mortgagor shall keep and maintain
the Said Flat and the building in good and tenantable condition
and repair and shall not commit or permit any waste or damage
thereto. The Mortgagor shall not make any structural alteration
or addition to the flat without the prior written approval of
the Mortgagee.
2.6 TAXES AND OUTGOINGS: The Mortgagor shall regularly and
punctually pay all municipal taxes, property tax, house tax,
water and sewerage charges, electricity charges, maintenance
charges payable to the Residents Welfare Association or any
authority, and all other rates, taxes, cesses, assessments and
outgoings of whatsoever nature in respect of the Mortgaged
Property and shall produce receipts of such payments to the
Bank on demand.
2.7 INSPECTION: The Mortgagor shall permit the Mortgagee, its
officers, representatives and agents to enter upon and inspect
the Mortgaged Property at all reasonable times upon reasonable
notice.
2.8 TITLE DOCUMENTS: The Mortgagor has deposited the original Sale
Deed No. 5847/2005 and all other title documents, including
the allotment letter, possession letter, approved building
plans, completion certificate, and mutation records pertaining
to the Mortgaged Property with the Mortgagee, and the same
shall remain deposited with the Mortgagee until the full and
final repayment of the loan.
2.9 INFORMATION: The Mortgagor shall promptly inform the Mortgagee
of any litigation, notice, order, proceeding, or claim
affecting the Mortgaged Property or the Mortgagor's title
thereto or his ability to repay the loan.
2.10 COMPLIANCE WITH LAWS: The Mortgagor shall comply with all
applicable laws, bye-laws, rules, regulations and orders of
any government, municipal, local or statutory authority with
respect to the Mortgaged Property.
3. EVENTS OF DEFAULT
The following shall constitute events of default under this Deed:
3.1 Failure by the Mortgagor to pay any EMI or any other sum due
hereunder within thirty (30) days of the due date;
3.2 Breach by the Mortgagor of any covenant, condition, warranty
or undertaking contained in this Deed or the Sanction Letter;
3.3 Any representation or warranty made by the Mortgagor herein or
in the loan application being found to be untrue, incorrect or
misleading in any material respect;
3.4 The Mortgagor being adjudicated as insolvent, or filing a
petition for insolvency, or any attachment, distress or
execution being levied against the Mortgaged Property;
3.5 The Mortgaged Property being acquired, requisitioned or
materially damaged by any cause;
3.6 Any other charge, mortgage, lien or encumbrance being created
on the Mortgaged Property without the prior written consent of
the Bank.
Upon the occurrence of any event of default, the entire outstanding
loan amount together with interest, costs, charges and expenses
shall immediately become due and payable, and the Mortgagee shall
be entitled to exercise all rights and remedies available to it
under this Deed, the Transfer of Property Act, 1882, the Code of
Civil Procedure, 1908, the Securitisation and Reconstruction of
Financial Assets and Enforcement of Security Interest Act, 2002
(SARFAESI Act), and any other applicable law.
4. SARFAESI ACT CLAUSE
4.1 The Mortgagor hereby acknowledges and agrees that the
Mortgagee, being a secured creditor within the meaning of the
Securitisation and Reconstruction of Financial Assets and
Enforcement of Security Interest Act, 2002 (hereinafter
"SARFAESI Act"), shall be entitled, without the intervention
of any court or tribunal, to enforce its security interest by
taking possession of the Mortgaged Property, taking over the
management of the Mortgaged Property, appointing a manager to
manage the same, or selling or otherwise disposing of the
Mortgaged Property, in accordance with the provisions of
Section 13 of the SARFAESI Act, upon the account of the
Mortgagor being classified as a Non-Performing Asset (NPA)
under the guidelines of the Reserve Bank of India.
4.2 The Mortgagor hereby waives any right to raise objections to
the exercise of the rights of the Mortgagee under the SARFAESI
Act, save and except such rights as may be available to the
Mortgagor under Section 17 of the SARFAESI Act before the
Debts Recovery Tribunal.
5. CERSAI REGISTRATION
The Mortgagor acknowledges and agrees that the Mortgagee shall be
entitled and is hereby authorized to register the charge/mortgage
created hereunder with the Central Registry of Securitisation Asset
Reconstruction and Security Interest of India (CERSAI) established
under Section 20 of the SARFAESI Act, 2002, and the Mortgagor shall
cooperate and provide all such information and documents as may be
required for the purpose of such registration. The cost of CERSAI
registration shall be borne by the Mortgagor.
6. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF THE MORTGAGOR
The Mortgagor hereby represents and warrants to the Mortgagee that:
6.1 He is the sole, absolute and lawful owner of the Mortgaged
Property and has good, clear, marketable and undisputed title
to the same, free from all encumbrances, charges, liens,
attachments, claims, demands and litigation of every kind;
6.2 The Mortgaged Property is not subject to any agreement, option,
right of pre-emption, trust, wakf, religious or charitable
endowment, tenancy, leave and licence, lease or any other
right in favour of any third party;
6.3 There are no arrears of taxes, duties, rates, cesses or
outgoings whatsoever in respect of the Mortgaged Property;
6.4 The Mortgaged Property is free from acquisition, requisition
or resumption proceedings by any government or statutory
authority;
6.5 The Mortgagor has full power and authority to mortgage the
Mortgaged Property in the manner herein contained;
6.6 All information and documents furnished by the Mortgagor to
the Mortgagee in connection with the loan are true, correct,
and complete.
7. INDEMNITY
The Mortgagor shall indemnify and keep indemnified the Mortgagee
against all losses, damages, costs, charges, expenses, claims, suits,
proceedings and demands of whatsoever nature arising from any breach
of the warranties, covenants or conditions contained herein or
arising from any defect in the title of the Mortgagor to the
Mortgaged Property or from any action taken by any person claiming
any right, title or interest in the Mortgaged Property.
8. STAMP DUTY AND REGISTRATION
This Mortgage Deed is executed on non-judicial stamp paper of
appropriate value as prescribed under the Indian Stamp Act, 1899 as
applicable in the National Capital Territory of Delhi and the Delhi
Stamp (Prevention of Under-Valuation of Instruments) Rules, 2007.
All stamp duty and registration charges shall be borne by the
Mortgagor. This Deed shall be registered at the Office of the
Sub-Registrar, SRO V-A (Hauz Khas), New Delhi, in accordance with
Section 17 of the Registration Act, 1908.
9. MISCELLANEOUS
9.1 GOVERNING LAW: This Deed shall be governed by and construed in
accordance with the laws of India. The courts at New Delhi
shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
9.2 NOTICES: All notices and communications under this Deed shall
be in writing and delivered personally or sent by registered
post / speed post to the addresses mentioned hereinabove.
9.3 SEVERABILITY: If any provision of this Deed is held to be
invalid, illegal or unenforceable, the remaining provisions
shall continue in full force and effect.
9.4 ENTIRE AGREEMENT: This Deed, together with the Sanction Letter
and other loan documents, constitutes the entire agreement
between the Parties with respect to the mortgage of the
Mortgaged Property.
