GIFT DEED
================================================================================
This GIFT DEED (hereinafter referred to as "this Deed") is executed and
entered into on this 14th day of February, 2015 (Fourteenth day of
February, Two Thousand and Fifteen) at New Delhi.
BETWEEN
MR. VIKRAM SINGH CHAUHAN, Son of Shri Harbhajan Singh Chauhan, aged about
48 years, residing at Flat No. 8, Fourth Floor, Plot No. 1A, Hauz Khas
Enclave, New Delhi - 110016, holding Permanent Account Number (PAN):
BKCPC8834M and Aadhaar No. XXXX-XXXX-4721 (hereinafter referred to as
the "DONOR", which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context or
meaning thereof, include his heirs, executors, administrators, legal
representatives and assigns) of the FIRST PART;
AND
MRS. PRIYA VIKRAM CHAUHAN, Wife of Mr. Vikram Singh Chauhan, aged about
44 years, residing at Flat No. 8, Fourth Floor, Plot No. 1A, Hauz Khas
Enclave, New Delhi - 110016, holding Permanent Account Number (PAN):
CJKPC2294L and Aadhaar No. XXXX-XXXX-5938 (hereinafter referred to as
the "DONEE", which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context or
meaning thereof, include her heirs, executors, administrators, legal
representatives and assigns) of the SECOND PART.
The DONOR and the DONEE are hereinafter collectively referred to as the
"Parties" and individually as a "Party".
RECITALS
WHEREAS the Donor is the lawful and absolute owner of the immovable
property being Flat No. 8, situated on the Fourth Floor, in the building
constructed on Plot No. 1A, Hauz Khas Enclave, New Delhi - 110016,
admeasuring 1200 sq.ft. (One Thousand Two Hundred Square Feet) of super
built-up area, more particularly described in the Schedule hereunder
written (hereinafter referred to as the "Said Property" or the
"Scheduled Property");
AND WHEREAS the Donor acquired the Said Property by virtue of a Sale Deed
bearing Document No. 5847 of the year 2005, registered in the Office of
the Sub-Registrar, SRO V-A (Hauz Khas), New Delhi, executed in his favour
by the original allottee, Shri Rameshwar Prasad Gupta, Son of Late Shri
Durga Prasad Gupta, for a total sale consideration of Rs. 42,00,000/-
(Rupees Forty-Two Lakhs Only);
AND WHEREAS subsequent to the said acquisition, the Donor had created an
equitable mortgage over the Said Property in favour of Axis
Bank, Hauz Khas Branch, New Delhi, by deposit of original title
deeds, to secure a housing loan of Rs. 20,00,000/- (Rupees Twenty Lakhs
Only);
AND WHEREAS the said housing loan has been fully repaid and discharged by
the Donor, and Axis Bank has executed a Deed of Reconveyance /
Release Deed bearing Document No. 14502 of the year 2012, registered in
the Office of the Sub-Registrar, SRO V-A (Hauz Khas), New Delhi,
releasing and reconveying all rights, title, and interest in the Said
Property back to the Donor, free from any lien, charge, or encumbrance
created in favour of the said Bank;
AND WHEREAS the Donor has been in peaceful and uninterrupted possession
and enjoyment of the Said Property since the date of its acquisition
in 2005, and the Said Property is presently free from all encumbrances,
liens, charges, mortgages, attachments, acquisitions, notifications,
claims, demands, lis pendens, court orders and liabilities whatsoever;
AND WHEREAS the Donee is the legally wedded wife of the Donor, and
the Donor and Donee have been married since the year 1994 and have been
residing together at the Said Property as their matrimonial home;
AND WHEREAS the Donor, out of natural love and affection which he bears
towards the Donee, being his lawfully wedded wife, and in consideration
of the Donee's devotion, companionship, and contribution to the family,
is desirous of making a gift of the Said Property to the Donee absolutely
and forever, without any monetary consideration, in accordance with the
provisions of Section 122 of the Transfer of Property Act, 1882;
AND WHEREAS the Donor hereby declares that this Gift is being made
voluntarily, without any coercion, undue influence, fraud, or
misrepresentation, and with full knowledge and understanding of its
legal consequences;
AND WHEREAS the Donee has expressed her willingness to accept the gift
of the Said Property from the Donor during the lifetime of the Donor,
as required under Section 122 of the Transfer of Property Act, 1882;
AND WHEREAS the Said Property falls within the municipal limits of the
Municipal Corporation of Delhi (MCD) and is subject to property tax
levied by MCD, and all property tax dues up to the date of this Deed
have been fully paid and discharged by the Donor;
AND WHEREAS all other outgoings including water charges, electricity
charges, maintenance charges payable to the Residents' Welfare
Association and any other statutory dues have been paid up to date
by the Donor.
NOW THIS GIFT DEED WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:
1. OPERATIVE CLAUSE - GIFT AND TRANSFER
That in consideration of the natural love and affection which the
Donor bears towards the Donee, being his lawfully wedded wife, and
for no monetary consideration whatsoever, the Donor hereby voluntarily,
unconditionally, and irrevocably grants, conveys, transfers and
assigns by way of gift, the Said Property described in the Schedule
hereunder written, together with all rights, titles, interests,
claims, and demands of the Donor in, to, and upon the Said Property,
including all easements, privileges, advantages, and appurtenances
thereunto belonging or in any way appertaining thereto, unto and
to the use of the Donee absolutely and forever, TO HAVE AND TO HOLD
the Said Property unto the Donee, her heirs, executors,
administrators, and assigns absolutely and forever.
2. ACCEPTANCE BY DONEE
That the Donee hereby accepts the gift of the Said Property made by
the Donor during the lifetime of the Donor, as required under
Section 122 of the Transfer of Property Act, 1882. The Donee
acknowledges that the gift is made voluntarily and out of natural
love and affection, and that no monetary consideration has been
paid or is payable by the Donee to the Donor for this transfer.
3. DELIVERY OF POSSESSION
That the Donor hereby delivers and hands over the actual, physical,
and legal possession of the Said Property to the Donee on the date
of execution of this Deed. The Donee acknowledges receipt of such
possession and shall henceforth be entitled to use, occupy, enjoy,
and deal with the Said Property in any manner she deems fit,
including but not limited to selling, leasing, mortgaging, or
otherwise alienating the same.
4. IRREVOCABILITY
That this Gift Deed is irrevocable in nature and the Donor shall
have no right, title, or interest in the Said Property from the
date of execution of this Deed. The Donor hereby relinquishes all
claims, rights, and interests in the Said Property in favour of
the Donee absolutely and forever.
5. COVENANTS AND WARRANTIES OF THE DONOR
The Donor hereby covenants, represents, and warrants to the Donee
as follows:
(a) That the Donor is the sole and absolute owner of the Said
Property and has clear, marketable and unencumbered title to
the same, and has full legal right, power, and authority to
make this gift;
(b) That the Said Property is free from all encumbrances, charges,
liens, mortgages, attachments, claims, demands, acquisitions,
notifications, lis pendens, court orders, and liabilities of
whatsoever nature;
(c) That the Donor has not entered into any agreement, arrangement,
or understanding with any person or entity for the sale,
transfer, exchange, or other disposition of the Said Property;
(d) That the Donor has not created any tenancy, lease, license,
or any right of occupation in favour of any third party in
respect of the Said Property;
(e) That there is no litigation, suit, proceeding, or dispute
pending or threatened before any court, tribunal, or authority
in respect of the Said Property;
(f) That all taxes, rates, charges, cesses, and other statutory
levies payable in respect of the Said Property have been duly
paid up to the date of this Deed;
(g) That the Said Property is not subject to any acquisition,
requisition, or notification under any statute or law for the
time being in force;
(h) That in the event of any defect in the title of the Said
Property or any claim or demand being made by any person
whomsoever, the Donor shall indemnify and keep the Donee
indemnified and harmless against all losses, damages, costs,
charges, and expenses that the Donee may suffer or incur.
6. STAMP DUTY AND EXPENSES
That the stamp duty of Rs. 1,50,000/- (Rupees One Lakh Fifty
Thousand Only), computed on the basis of the circle rate applicable
to the area as notified by the Government of NCT of Delhi, and the
registration fee of Rs. 15,000/- (Rupees Fifteen Thousand Only)
payable under the Indian Stamp Act, 1899 (as applicable to the
NCT of Delhi) and the Registration Act, 1908 respectively, have
been borne and paid by the Donor.
7. MUTATION AND RECORDS
That the Donee shall be entitled to get the Said Property mutated
in her name in the records of the concerned revenue authorities,
development authorities (DDA), local bodies, cooperative societies,
and any other authorities as may be required, and the Donor shall
provide all necessary cooperation and sign all documents as may
be required for the purpose.
8. GENERAL
(a) This Deed shall be governed by and construed in accordance with
the laws of India, including the Transfer of Property Act, 1882,
the Indian Stamp Act, 1899, and the Registration Act, 1908.
(b) The Courts at New Delhi shall have exclusive jurisdiction in
respect of any matter arising out of or in connection with
this Deed.
(c) This Deed constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties
with respect to the subject matter hereof.
