WHEREAS
A. WHEREAS the Delhi Development Authority is the owner and is seized and possessed of the
land comprised in Plot No. 1A, Hauz Khas Enclave, New Delhi - 110016, and has developed the
said land under the Hauz Khas Residential Scheme, 1988, sanctioned under Section 12 of the
Delhi Development Act, 1957, and has constructed thereupon a residential multi-storeyed
apartment building containing multiple self-contained residential flats for allotment to the
general public.
B. AND WHEREAS the DDA, in pursuance of its aforesaid residential scheme and in exercise of
the powers conferred upon it under Sections 12 and 22 of the Delhi Development Act, 1957 read
with the DDA (Disposal of Developed Nazul Land) Rules, 1981, invited applications from eligible
persons for allotment of residential flats in the said Hauz Khas Residential Scheme, 1988, and
the Grantee, Mr. Rajesh Kumar Sharma, duly applied for the allotment of a residential flat and
was selected through the computerized draw of lots conducted by the DDA on 05-12-1989.
C. AND WHEREAS the Delhi Development Authority issued a Demand-cum-Allotment Letter bearing
No. F.7(42)/88/HK/DDA dated 12th March 1990 in favour of the Grantee, allotting Flat No. 8,
Fourth Floor, Plot No. 1A, Hauz Khas Enclave, New Delhi - 110016, admeasuring 1200 sq.ft.
(super built-up area) and 950 sq.ft. (carpet area), at a total cost of Rs. 6,85,000/- (Rupees
Six Lakhs Eighty-Five Thousand Only) on a leasehold basis for a period of 99 (Ninety-Nine)
years, perpetually renewable, subject to the terms and conditions mentioned therein and in
accordance with the provisions of the Delhi Development Act, 1957.
D. AND WHEREAS the Grantee has paid the entire cost and consideration of the said residential
flat aggregating Rs. 6,85,000/- (Rupees Six Lakhs Eighty-Five Thousand Only) to the DDA, the
details of which are as follows: (i) Initial deposit of Rs. 50,000/- vide Demand Draft No.
342156 dated 15-01-1990 drawn on State Bank of India, Parliament Street Branch; (ii) Balance
amount of Rs. 6,35,000/- paid in instalments between 01-04-1990 and 30-11-1994, full and final
receipts whereof having been duly acknowledged by the Delhi Development Authority vide Receipt
No. DDA/HK/1994/R-4278 dated 30-11-1994, and the DDA has no outstanding dues from the Grantee.
E. AND WHEREAS the Grantee has requested the DDA to execute and register a formal Conveyance
Deed in his favour in respect of the said residential flat, and the DDA, being satisfied that
all terms and conditions of the allotment have been complied with, has agreed to execute this
Conveyance Deed in favour of the Grantee.
NOW THIS CONVEYANCE DEED WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:
1. That in consideration of the sum of Rs. 6,85,000/- (Rupees Six Lakhs Eighty-Five Thousand
Only) already paid by the Grantee to the DDA (the receipt whereof the DDA doth hereby
acknowledge), and in further consideration of the covenants and conditions herein contained,
the Delhi Development Authority doth hereby grant, convey, transfer, and assign unto the
Grantee ALL THAT the residential flat more particularly described in the Schedule of Property
hereunder written, together with all rights, title, interest, easements, privileges, and
appurtenances whatsoever belonging to or in any way appertaining to the said flat, TO HAVE AND
TO HOLD the same unto and to the use of the Grantee on a leasehold basis for a period of
Ninety-Nine (99) years commencing from the 12th day of March, 1990, perpetually renewable
thereafter, subject to the payment of ground rent as may be fixed by the DDA from time to time.
2. The DDA hereby covenants with the Grantee as follows:
(a) That the DDA has full right, power, and authority to grant, convey, and transfer the said
residential flat unto the Grantee in the manner aforesaid.
(b) That the Grantee shall peacefully and quietly hold, possess, and enjoy the said flat
without any interruption, disturbance, claim, or demand whatsoever from the DDA or any person
claiming under or through the DDA.
(c) That the said residential flat is free from all encumbrances, charges, liens, attachments,
and claims of whatsoever nature.
3. The Grantee hereby covenants with the DDA as follows:
(a) That the Grantee shall use the said flat exclusively for residential purposes and shall not
convert the same for any commercial or other non-residential use without the prior written
permission of the Delhi Development Authority.
(b) That the Grantee shall not make any structural alterations or additions to the said flat
without the prior written approval of the DDA.
(c) That the Grantee shall pay the ground rent, maintenance charges, property tax, and all
other levies and charges as may be applicable from time to time.
(d) That the Grantee shall not transfer, assign, or part with the possession of the said flat
within a period of five years from the date of allotment without the prior written permission
of the DDA.
(e) That the Grantee shall abide by all the terms and conditions of the allotment letter and
the bye-laws framed by the DDA.
4. It is hereby agreed and declared that this Conveyance Deed is subject to the provisions of
the Delhi Development Act, 1957, the Registration Act, 1908, the Indian Stamp Act, 1899, and
the Transfer of Property Act, 1882, and the rules and regulations framed thereunder, as amended
from time to time.
