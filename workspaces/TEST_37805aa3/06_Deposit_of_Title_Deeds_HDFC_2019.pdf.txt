NOW THIS MEMORANDUM WITNESSETH AND IT IS HEREBY AGREED, DECLARED AND
RECORDED AS FOLLOWS:
1. CREATION OF EQUITABLE MORTGAGE
That the Mortgagor has on this day, i.e. 20th June, 2019, deposited
the original title deeds and documents listed hereinabove with the
Bank at its Hauz Khas Branch, New Delhi, with the intent to create
a mortgage by deposit of title deeds (equitable mortgage) under
Section 58(f) of the Transfer of Property Act, 1882, as security
for the due repayment of the Loan of Rs. 55,00,000/- (Rupees
Fifty-Five Lakhs Only) together with all interest (including
penal interest, if any), costs, charges, expenses, and all other
monies that may become due and payable by the Mortgagor to the
Bank under or in connection with the Loan Agreement.
2. EXTENT OF SECURITY
That this equitable mortgage shall be a continuing security and
shall remain in force until the Loan together with all interest,
costs, charges, expenses, and other monies have been fully and
finally repaid and discharged to the complete satisfaction of the
Bank, and the Bank issues a formal No Objection Certificate (NOC)
or Release Letter confirming the same.
3. COVENANTS OF THE MORTGAGOR
The Mortgagor hereby covenants, represents, and warrants to the
Bank as follows:
(a) That the Mortgagor is the sole and absolute owner of the
Mortgaged Property and has clear, marketable and unencumbered
title to the same;
(b) That the Mortgaged Property is free from all prior
encumbrances, charges, liens, mortgages, attachments, claims,
demands, acquisitions, notifications, lis pendens, court
orders, and liabilities of whatsoever nature;
(c) That the Mortgagor has full legal right, power, and authority
to create this mortgage and to deposit the title deeds with
the Bank;
(d) That the Mortgagor shall not sell, transfer, assign, lease,
sublease, license, gift, exchange, or otherwise alienate or
create any third-party interest in the Mortgaged Property
without the prior written consent of the Bank;
(e) That the Mortgagor shall keep the Mortgaged Property insured
against fire, earthquake, flood, and other risks as may be
required by the Bank, and shall assign the insurance policy
in favour of the Bank;
(f) That the Mortgagor shall pay all taxes, rates, cesses,
charges, and other statutory levies payable in respect of
the Mortgaged Property punctually and regularly;
(g) That the Mortgagor shall maintain the Mortgaged Property in
good and tenantable condition and shall not make any
structural alterations without the prior written consent of
the Bank;
(h) That in the event of default in repayment of the Loan or
any instalment thereof, or breach of any term or condition
of the Loan Agreement or this Memorandum, the Bank shall be
entitled to enforce the security by sale or otherwise in
accordance with the provisions of the Transfer of Property
Act, 1882, the Securitisation and Reconstruction of Financial
Assets and Enforcement of Security Interest Act, 2002
(SARFAESI Act), and any other applicable law;
(i) That the Mortgagor shall indemnify and keep the Bank
indemnified and harmless against all losses, damages, costs,
charges, claims, demands, and expenses that the Bank may
suffer or incur by reason of any defect in the title of the
Mortgaged Property or any misrepresentation by the Mortgagor.
4. RIGHTS OF THE BANK
(a) The Bank shall retain the original title deeds and documents
deposited hereunder until the Loan together with all monies
due thereunder has been fully repaid and discharged;
(b) The Bank shall have the right to register this Memorandum
with the Sub-Registrar or any other authority as may be
required under applicable law;
(c) The Bank shall have the right to inspect the Mortgaged
Property at any time during the subsistence of the Loan;
(d) In the event of default, the Bank shall be entitled to take
possession of the Mortgaged Property and to sell or dispose
of the same in accordance with the applicable law, including
the SARFAESI Act, 2002.
5. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION
This Memorandum shall be governed by and construed in accordance
with the laws of India, including the Transfer of Property Act,
1882, the Indian Contract Act, 1872, the Indian Stamp Act, 1899,
the Registration Act, 1908, and the SARFAESI Act, 2002. The Courts
and Debt Recovery Tribunals at New Delhi shall have exclusive
jurisdiction.
