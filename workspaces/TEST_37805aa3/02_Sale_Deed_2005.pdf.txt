WHEREAS
A. WHEREAS the Delhi Development Authority (DDA), being the owner and developer of the
residential apartments constructed under the Hauz Khas Residential Scheme, 1988, on Plot No.
1A, Hauz Khas Enclave, New Delhi - 110016, allotted Flat No. 8, Fourth Floor, to the Vendor,
Mr. Rajesh Kumar Sharma, vide Demand-cum-Allotment Letter No. F.7(42)/88/HK/DDA dated 12th
March, 1990, on a leasehold basis for a period of 99 years, perpetually renewable, under the
provisions of the Delhi Development Act, 1957.
B. AND WHEREAS the Vendor duly paid the entire cost of the said residential flat amounting to
Rs. 6,85,000/- (Rupees Six Lakhs Eighty-Five Thousand Only) to the DDA and a formal Conveyance
Deed was executed and registered in favour of the Vendor by the Delhi Development Authority,
bearing Document No. 123 for the year 1995, registered at the Office of the Sub-Registrar, SRO
V-A (Hauz Khas), New Delhi, on the 18th day of January, 1995, in Book No. I, Volume No. 1247,
Pages 42 to 58.
C. AND WHEREAS the Vendor has been in continuous, uninterrupted, and peaceful possession and
enjoyment of the said residential flat since the date of the DDA allotment in 1990 and has been
residing therein with his family, and the said flat stands in the name of the Vendor in the
records of the DDA, the New Delhi Municipal Council (NDMC), and all other relevant authorities.
D. AND WHEREAS the Vendor has obtained the requisite No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the
Delhi Development Authority vide Letter No. F.7(42)/88/HK/DDA/NOC/2004-1187 dated 15th
December, 2004, permitting the transfer and sale of the said residential flat, the restriction
period of five years from the date of allotment having long expired.
E. AND WHEREAS the Vendor has represented and assured the Vendee that the said flat on the
fourth floor is free from all encumbrances, charges, liens, mortgages, prior sales, gifts,
attachments, acquisitions, trusts, disputes, litigation, claims, and demands of whatsoever
nature, and the Vendor has clear, marketable, and undisputed title to the said flat.
F. AND WHEREAS the Vendee, having satisfied himself as to the clear title of the Vendor and
the physical condition and legal status of the said residential flat, has agreed to purchase
the same from the Vendor for a total sale consideration of Rs. 42,50,000/- (Rupees Forty-Two
Lakhs Fifty Thousand Only), and the Vendor has agreed to sell, convey, and transfer the same to
the Vendee on the terms and conditions hereinafter appearing.
NOW THIS SALE DEED WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:
1. That in consideration of the sum of Rs. 42,50,000/- (Rupees Forty-Two Lakhs Fifty Thousand
Only) paid by the Vendee to the Vendor, the receipt and sufficiency whereof the Vendor doth
hereby acknowledge, the payment having been made as follows:
(a) Rs. 5,00,000/- (Rupees Five Lakhs Only) paid as earnest money / advance on 10th January
2005, vide cheque No. 435218 dated 10-01-2005, drawn on ICICI Bank, Vasant Vihar Branch, New
Delhi;
(b) Rs. 37,50,000/- (Rupees Thirty-Seven Lakhs Fifty Thousand Only) paid at the time of
execution and registration of this Sale Deed, vide Demand Draft No. 782341 dated 20-03-2005,
drawn on HDFC Bank, Hauz Khas Branch, New Delhi, in favour of Mr. Rajesh Kumar Sharma;
the Vendor doth hereby sell, convey, transfer, and assign unto the Vendee ALL THAT the
residential flat more particularly described in the Schedule of Property hereunder written,
together with all rights, title, interest, easements, privileges, and appurtenances whatsoever
belonging to or in any way appertaining to the said flat, TO HAVE AND TO HOLD the same unto the
Vendee absolutely and forever, subject to the terms and conditions of the original DDA
allotment and the leasehold tenure.
2. VENDOR'S REPRESENTATIONS, WARRANTIES AND COVENANTS:
The Vendor hereby represents, warrants, and covenants with the Vendee as follows:
(a) That the Vendor is the sole and absolute owner of the said residential flat and has full
right, power, and authority to sell, convey, and transfer the same to the Vendee.
(b) That the said flat is free from all encumbrances, charges, liens, mortgages, prior sales,
gifts, exchange, partition, attachments, acquisitions, trusts, court orders, decrees,
injunctions, and claims of whatsoever nature, and no person other than the Vendor has any
right, title, interest, or claim in the said flat.
(c) That the Vendor has not entered into any agreement, arrangement, or understanding with any
other person for the sale, transfer, mortgage, or disposal of the said flat, and this Sale Deed
constitutes the first and only transfer of the flat from the Vendor.
(d) That all dues, taxes, charges, levies, and assessments payable in respect of the said flat,
including but not limited to property tax, house tax, water and electricity charges, DDA ground
rent, maintenance charges, and all other statutory and municipal levies, have been duly paid by
the Vendor up to the date of this Sale Deed, and no arrears are outstanding.
(e) That the Vendor has not done or suffered anything whereby the said flat or any part thereof
has been or may be encumbered, charged, or affected in any manner whatsoever.
(f) That the Vendor shall, at his own cost, execute and register all such further deeds,
documents, and writings as may be required by the Vendee for the purpose of more fully and
effectively transferring, assuring, and confirming the said flat unto the Vendee.
(g) That the Vendor has delivered and handed over all original title documents, records, and
papers pertaining to the said flat to the Vendee, including but not limited to the original DDA
allotment letter, DDA Conveyance Deed, DDA NOC for transfer, payment receipts, and possession
letter.
(h) That the Vendor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Vendee from and against all
actions, claims, demands, proceedings, costs, charges, expenses, losses, and damages which the
Vendee may suffer or incur by reason of any defect in the title of the Vendor or by reason of
any encumbrance or claim affecting the said flat.
3. DELIVERY OF POSSESSION:
The Vendor hereby confirms that he has delivered actual, physical, vacant, and peaceful
possession of the said residential flat on the fourth floor to the Vendee on the date of
execution of this Sale Deed, i.e., 22nd March, 2005, along with all fittings, fixtures, keys,
and appurtenances, and the Vendee confirms having received the same.
4. INDEMNITY CLAUSE:
The Vendor hereby covenants and agrees to indemnify and keep indemnified the Vendee, his heirs,
executors, administrators, and assigns, against all claims, demands, suits, proceedings, costs,
charges, and expenses arising out of or in connection with any defect in the title of the
Vendor or any breach of the representations, warranties, and covenants contained herein. This
indemnity shall survive the execution and registration of this Sale Deed.
5. MISCELLANEOUS:
(a) This Sale Deed shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the provisions of the
Transfer of Property Act, 1882, the Registration Act, 1908, the Indian Stamp Act, 1899, and all
other applicable laws of India.
(b) Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Sale Deed shall be subject to the
exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts of New Delhi.
(c) The Vendee shall get the mutation of the said flat recorded in his name in the records of
the DDA, NDMC, and all other relevant authorities at his own cost and expense.
